Information About Trojan


This is a simulation of an old and popular trojan called you are an Idiot. The aim of this trojan was destroying your ram by creating new html pages and duplicate it when you try to close it.
Unlike modern malware that steals your data or locks your files, this virus didn't care about destruction—it just wanted to make fun of you and mess up your screen.
Evry time you press Alt-F4 or try to press the close button or just try to close it from every possible way it duplicates itself again and again until it crashes your pc .
As soon as you opened it, black-and-white smiling faces started flashing on the screen while a catchy, annoying loop played: *"You are an idiot! hahahahhahaaha"*
Trying to close the pop-ups manually was a trap. The only way to stop it on old systems like Windows XP was opening the Task Manager to kill `iexplore.exe` or straight up pulling the power plug.
